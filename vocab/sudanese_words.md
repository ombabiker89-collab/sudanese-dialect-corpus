# Sudanese Vocabulary

## Common words
- شنو = what
- كيف = how
- كسوم = rude/vulgar (instead of وقح)
- زول = man/guy
- بت = girl
- دايره = want/like
- ما دايره = don't want
- اشطة = nice / good / cool (Egyptian loanword used in Sudanese speech)
- سامحة = beautiful / pretty (compliment for a woman)
- حلوة شديد = very beautiful / very pretty
- ما عندنا = we don't have
- ما عندناكم ايش = we don't have anything (colloquial)
- وقفة حمارة = donkey stop / long pause (humorous)
- يا بتة = hey girl (affectionate/teasing)

## Regional notes
- Khartoum: more standard Sudanese
- Darfur: some unique terms
- East: influences from Beja etc.

## Notes from conversation
- سامحة is used as a compliment meaning beautiful/pretty, often with clothing or appearance.
- اشطة is borrowed from Egyptian but commonly used in casual Sudanese speech to mean nice/good.
- حلوة شديد is a strong compliment for a woman.
